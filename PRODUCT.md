# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Style-conscious padel players and club members shopping for refined performance apparel that moves naturally between competitive play and post-match social settings.

## Product Purpose

WON OF ONE presents and sells a tightly edited capsule of premium padel apparel. The storefront should make the sport, its glass-court setting, and its Riviera club culture immediately recognizable while conveying technical performance and understated European tailoring.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, sun-warmed, and exacting. The brand should feel confident and cultivated without becoming formal, nostalgic, or ostentatious.

## Anti-references

Generic gym culture, conventional tennis-club imagery, neon mass-market sportswear, unrelated fashion editorials, and luxury signals that have no visible connection to padel.

## Design Principles

1. Show the sport before explaining it.
2. Let technical credibility and social ease coexist.
3. Use restraint to focus attention, not to remove context.
4. Make every lifestyle detail feel grounded in real padel culture.
5. Keep the collection edited, tactile, and specific.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve strong text contrast over photography, meaningful alternative text, keyboard-visible controls, responsive image crops, and reduced-motion preferences.
